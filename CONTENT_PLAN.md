## Content rules (apply to every post)

1. Open with a 40–60 word answer-first block (AEO/AI extraction).
2. H2s phrased as questions; answer in 2–4 sentences before expanding.
3. Include: distance/duration (as approximate ranges), practical booking steps, comparison tables where relevant.
4. Cite authoritative sources (Saudi Railways / Haramain, airport authority, etc.) with real URLs.
5. Add "Last updated: [date]" near the title.
6. End with a clear CTA: "Book your transfer via WhatsApp".
7. Unique content — no copy-paste with swapped cities. Run similarity check in `validate-seo.ts`.
